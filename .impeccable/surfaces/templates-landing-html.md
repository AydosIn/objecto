# Surface brief: Object Biographies public site (The Recap)

Scope: every public, non-admin template (landing, account pages, profile, upload, exhibit, delete). Visitor mode: Persuade on the landing page; Operate on account, profile and upload; Experience on the exhibit page. Audience: portfolio viewers and friends on iPhone (sometimes Mac); job: snap an object, read what it says about you, laugh, screenshot it. Constraints: Django templates, plain CSS and vanilla JS, no build step; installable to the home screen; lowercase texting voice; no invented claims (example content is labelled as an example).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every object gets its own recap: one loud color, one fact, one screen at a time. Refuses the centred-hero, app-icon, three-feature-cards app page.

OWN-WORLD: Eight drenched fields with paired ink (violet #5B2BFF, hot pink #FF2E7E, lime #C8F031, traffic orange #FF6A13, pool blue #1FA2FF, butter #FFD43B, mint #2BE0A0, ink #111111); each exhibit snaps its photo's colour to the ramp ("aura"). SF Pro Display Heavy, lowercase, tight. Liquid Glass bars and sheets, story progress segments, photo tickets, white capsule actions, springy motion.

STORY: The visitor sees an example object roast its owner, gets that the roast comes from the photo, and taps "make your stuff talk".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing: a full-bleed colour slide autoplays an example recap of gerald the mug; progress segments sit under the glass bar, a photo ticket marks it as an example, a giant left-aligned statement fills the middle, a tilted photo card sits low, and a white capsule "make your stuff talk" stays pinned at the bottom with "log in" beside it.

FORM: The Recap, position 5 of 7 on my ordered list (assigned by the roll; raised by darkroom, VU meter, Tanaka and bazaar disciplines). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the exhibit recap, colour slides you tap or swipe through with springy giant type, ending on a summary card. Motion grammar: springs with overshoot for arrivals, fast exits, colour wipes between slides; reduced motion falls back to crossfades.

Unresolved: museum grid (Phase 7) will inherit the aura tiles.
